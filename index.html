A full semester in Berlin, fully covered.

The International Excellence Scholarship places outstanding students from 21 countries at one of Berlin's top universities for a complete exchange semester — combined with a professional internship of at least 8 weeks. Tuition, housing, and daily costs are covered depending on your funding tier.

  • Bachelor's, Master's, or Doctorate students in one of the 21 eligible countries
  • Age 28 or under when the scholarship period starts
  • English or German at B1 level or higher — no certificate needed at application stage
  • Strong academic record (GPA details in full application guidelines)
  • Full Scholarship — Pocket money, health insurance, accommodation, tuition, program fees. Only travel not covered.
  • Partial Scholarship Plus — Pocket money, accommodation, tuition and program fees. No health insurance.
  • Partial Scholarship — Pocket money and tuition/program fees only.
Academic
Semester · ~5 months

Study at Berlin's top universities.

Scholars join one of four prestigious Berlin institutions and attend regular courses alongside local students. Courses are taught in English or German depending on the programme.

  • Humboldt University of Berlin (HU)
  • Freie Universität Berlin (FU)
  • Berlin School of Economics and Law (HWR)
  • University of Potsdam
Internship
Min. 8 weeks · full-time

Professional internship in the heart of Europe.

After the semester, scholars complete a full-time internship at a Berlin-based company in their field of study. Copernicus Berlin supports placement through its employer network.

  • Minimum 8 weeks of full-time professional experience
  • Placement support via Copernicus Berlin's employer network
  • Access to Berlin's startup, NGO, research, and corporate ecosystem
  • Internship report required for scholarship completion
Full Tier
Maximum support

Full Scholarship — all costs covered.

The Full Scholarship tier is the most comprehensive award, covering nearly every expense during your time in Berlin. Only your travel to and from Germany is not included.

  • Monthly pocket money for living expenses
  • Student health insurance in Germany
  • Shared accommodation in a student residence
  • University enrollment and tuition fees
  • Program activities, city tours, and cultural events
  • Copernicus Berlin alumni network access
Partial Plus
Strong support

Partial Scholarship Plus — housing & tuition covered.

Partial Scholarship Plus covers housing and tuition but not health insurance. Scholars arrange their own coverage through affordable German student insurance providers.

  • ✔ Monthly pocket money
  • ✔ Accommodation in student residence
  • ✔ University enrollment and tuition fees
  • ✔ Program activities
  • ✘ Health insurance (self-arranged)
  • ✘ Travel costs
Step 1
15–20 min

Submit the online application form.

Complete the online form at copernicusberlin.org/ies. You will need your university enrollment proof, a short motivation statement (max 400 words), and contact details for one academic referee. No language certificate required at this stage.

Step 2
~1 week after shortlisting

Complete a short skills task.

Shortlisted candidates receive a written or practical skills task by email. This typically takes 2–4 hours and tests analytical thinking and communication skills in English or German.

Step 3
30 min video call

Attend a selection interview.

Finalists are invited to a 30-minute video interview with the Copernicus Berlin selection committee. The interview focuses on academic goals, motivation, and adaptability. Track your application status anytime from your online account.

Step 4
Decision & onboarding

Receive your award & prepare for Berlin.

Successful scholars are notified by email and receive a scholarship contract. Copernicus Berlin then provides a dedicated onboarding package covering visa guidance, accommodation check-in, and a pre-departure information session.

Humboldt University of Berlin

One of Germany's oldest research universities, consistently ranked among Europe's top 100. Strong programmes in humanities, social sciences, law, and natural sciences. Located in the historic Mitte district.

Freie Universität Berlin

A leading research university and member of the German Excellence Initiative. Known internationally for area studies, life sciences, and political science. Main campus is in the leafy Dahlem neighbourhood.

HWR Berlin
Business & Law

Berlin School of Economics and Law

Germany's largest state university of applied sciences for business and law. Highly practice-oriented programmes in management, public administration, and legal studies — with strong links to Berlin's corporate sector.

Uni Potsdam
Founded 1991

University of Potsdam

Located just outside Berlin in the picturesque city of Potsdam (UNESCO World Heritage). Excellent programmes in cognitive science, law, economics, and education. Well connected by S-Bahn.
